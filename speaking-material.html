托福口语｜高复用总论点与例子

核心原则：同类题尽量用同一句，只改主语、对象和题目细节。

PART 1｜高复用总论点

使用方法：先直接回答题目，再从下面选 2–4 句最相关的句子。不要每题都重新造句。

总论点

固定句子

A｜减压、放松与专注

• It helps me relax and clear my mind.

• It also helps me reduce stress and feel calmer and more in control.

• When I feel less stressed, I can focus on one thing at a time.

• This helps me understand things better（worse） and make fewer （more）mistakes.

B｜健康、精力与稳定状态

• It is good for my physical health and gives me more energy.

• It also helps my body recover and keeps me from getting tired too easily.

• With more energy, I can stay focused for longer.

• It also makes it easier for me to keep a healthy routine.

C｜节省时间与便利

• It saves me a lot of time and makes my daily life easier.

• I don't have to spend too much time on unnecessary things.

• The time I save can be used for studying, relaxing, or spending time with people I care about.

• So my schedule becomes more flexible.

D｜省钱、预算与理性消费

• It helps me save money and avoid unnecessary spending.

• I can think more carefully before I spend my money.

• This helps me use my money more wisely.

• In this way, I can choose the best option that fits both my needs and my budget.

E｜成长、技能与自信

• It gives me a chance to learn something new and improve my skills.

• With more practice, I can see what I need to improve.

• As I make progress, I become more confident.

• Over time, I can handle new problems more independently.

F｜关系与交流

• It gives us a good chance to spend quality time together.

• We can talk, share our experiences, and understand each other better.

• This helps us build trust and strengthen our relationship.

• Over time, it can bring us closer.

G｜拓宽眼界与不同观点

• It gives me a chance to learn new things and hear different ideas.

• I can see the same thing from different perspectives.

• This helps me become more open-minded.

• It also helps me understand how other people think.

H｜社会、环保与公共影响

• It can help reduce pollution and protect the environment.

• Small actions can make a real difference when many people do them together.

• It can also make the community cleaner and more comfortable.

• Over time, these changes can benefit more people.

G 影响和不影响

•It may lead to (can avoid)constant distractions.

• This helps(makes) me understand things better（worse） and make fewer （more）mistakes.
