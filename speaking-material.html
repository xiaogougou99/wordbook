第一部分｜Speaking 第一题：经历与习惯

1. 只背三种骨架

题型

可直接使用的骨架

习惯/频率

I [do it] [frequency], mainly because [reason]. For example, [time/place/action]. This [observable result]. So it has become a practical part of my routine.

过去经历

One memorable experience was [event]. I was with [person] at [place], and we [two actions]. What made it meaningful was [detail]. As a result, [feeling/change].

偏好/个人看法

I prefer / value [choice], mainly because [reason]. For example, [specific case]. [Visible result]. The other option can work when [neutral situation], but [choice] fits my needs better.

一题只需要：直接回答＋一个主要理由＋一个可看见的细节＋结果。不要连续堆三个抽象理由，也不要把另一种选择说成错误。

2. 先准备这8组高复用经历

A｜徒步＋朋友＋摄影（最高复用）

可覆盖：公园、旅行、运动、放松、朋友、摄影、周末、自然

核心版本｜Last month, I spent a Saturday on a forest trail with two friends. We walked for three hours, took photos of the lake, and talked without checking our phones. As a result, I returned to campus feeling calm and focused.

同一例子的其他说法

The walk gave me a genuine break from academic pressure and helped me return to my work with a clearer mind.

Taking photos made me notice small changes in light and color, so the activity was both physical and creative.

Sharing the trail gave us time to talk naturally, which made the experience more memorable and strengthened our friendship.

Having that kind of flexibility made it easy to keep a healthy routine without feeling drained.

B｜钢琴＋音乐＋表达（艺术核心）

可覆盖：音乐频率、艺术、创造力、爱好、目标、学习技能、减压

核心版本｜I practice the piano several evenings a week. A familiar piece helps me slow down after class, while learning a difficult section gives me a clear sense of progress. Music can capture delicate inner feelings and express a personal perspective without words.

同一例子的其他说法

The visible progress gives me both a sense of achievement and a healthy way to express emotion.

Quiet instrumental music blocks distracting conversations and helps me stay focused.

Even fifteen minutes of practice gives me a genuine break from academic pressure and leaves me calmer afterward.

C｜网购＋课程表＋预算

可覆盖：网购频率、科技、时间管理、预算、便利、近期购买

核心版本｜I shop online more frequently because it fits my class schedule and student budget. For example, I can reorder notebooks in five minutes and compare several prices before paying. This saves time and keeps me from making rushed purchases.

同一例子的其他说法

For routine purchases, online reordering and price comparison save travel and waiting time.

A saved shopping list helps me avoid unnecessary items and stay within my monthly budget.

The main advantage is flexibility: I can complete the purchase between classes instead of rearranging my day.

D｜实体店＋试穿＋当场确认

可覆盖：买衣服、鞋、眼镜、近期购物、可靠选择、服务

核心版本｜I prefer testing an item before spending money. I recently tried on three pairs of running shoes and noticed that only one supported my feet comfortably. As a result, I avoided a return and chose a product I could use that day.

同一例子的其他说法

For clothing, shoes, or glasses, direct fitting and staff adjustment reduce sizing mistakes and unnecessary returns.

An employee compared two editions of a course book and found a student discount, so I saved money and bought the correct version.

Because the store was well organized and not crowded, I finished in about twenty minutes and stayed within my budget.

E｜固定作息＋第二天专注

可覆盖：睡眠、健康、早睡/晚睡、学习效率、规律习惯

核心版本｜I try to keep a fixed bedtime on school nights. It lets me review notes before class and stay focused during lectures. In contrast, a late night makes me slow and distracted the next day, while a predictable morning routine keeps me from feeling rushed.

同一例子的其他说法

A consistent schedule gives my body time to recover and makes my energy level more stable.

When I am well rested, I can finish difficult work with fewer careless mistakes.

The routine is simple, but its benefits accumulate because I repeat it every day.

F｜日历＋三件事清单

可覆盖：时间管理、组织、目标、学习、财务计划、压力

核心版本｜Every evening, I check my calendar and write down the three most important tasks for the next day. This gives me a clear starting point and keeps small deadlines from becoming last-minute emergencies. Reviewing the list also lets me adjust calmly when something unexpected happens.

同一例子的其他说法

Breaking a large goal into visible weekly steps makes progress easier to measure.

I leave some open time in the schedule, so the plan remains practical rather than exhausting.

The system reduces mental clutter because I do not have to remember every task at once.

集中注意力的四句套话：It helps me stay focused and makes me less likely to get distracted by things happening around me.

When I can concentrate on one thing at a time, I usually understand it better and work more efficiently.

It also keeps me from switching to unrelated activities, so I can use my time in a more organized way.

As a result, I can get more things done and feel more productive throughout the day.

G｜共同活动＋自然聊天

可覆盖：家人、朋友、节日、聚餐、共同爱好、支持

核心版本｜Spending time together gives people a chance to talk, share experiences, and understand one another better. Talking about daily tasks, something interesting that happened, or even a small worry can relieve stress and build closer bonds.

同一例子的其他说法

A simple shared activity can create lasting memories and strengthen the relationship over time.

Doing the activity together makes the whole experience warmer, more enjoyable, and more memorable.

When I do the same activity alone, there is no one to share the scenery or an exciting moment with.

H｜学习新技能＋可见进步

可覆盖：学习新技能、设置目标、可见进步、拓宽眼界、理解不同文化、创造力、自信、解决问题

核心版本｜I chose one small skill and practiced it consistently instead of trying to improve everything at once. Keeping a simple record made the progress visible, and each small improvement gave me more confidence to continue.

同一例子的其他说法

The experience taught me to break a difficult task into manageable steps.

When a problem appeared, I tested one solution at a time and learned from the result.

What I learned is useful now and can also support future study or work.

设置目标｜通用句

Setting a clear and realistic goal gives me a specific direction instead of leaving me with only a vague intention.

Breaking [GOAL] into small weekly steps makes my progress easier to measure and keeps me motivated.

Giving myself a practical deadline creates useful pressure and helps me turn a plan into consistent action.

Reaching each short-term milestone gives me a sense of achievement and encourages me to continue.

拓宽眼界｜通用句

Learning [SKILL OR SUBJECT] exposes me to new ideas and different ways of thinking.

More importantly, it broadens my horizons by showing me how other people approach the same problem.

It allows me to see a familiar problem from another perspective instead of relying on the same solution every time.

By communicating with people from different backgrounds, I can understand how culture and experience shape their choices.

Over time, this wider perspective makes me more open-minded, adaptable, and confident in unfamiliar situations.

The calm feeling is not temporary; it also helps me stay focused during the rest of the day.

环境与社区

方向B的特点

考场必须临场换的内容

题目要求的人：I / my friend / my family member。

可观察细节：时间、地点、同行者、两项动作、一个结果。

频率与数量：daily / twice a month / for twenty minutes。

具体物品或活动：notebooks、running shoes、one piano piece、a forest trail。

结论必须回扣题目，而不是机械重复万能句。

高分文本的必要条件：完整回答题目、细节具体、逻辑清楚、语言自然。固定句只能降低卡顿，不能替代发音、语速、停顿和临场任务完成度。
