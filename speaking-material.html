TOEFL SPEAKING

口语通用表达与高复用例子

整套材料保留七条结果链、四种答题结构和七个故事世界。先辨认题型，再选一条结果链或一个故事；方括号内必须换成题目内容，不要把两个完整模块硬塞进同一道题。

最省背诵量的顺序：先背A减压专注、C时间便利、E成长能力、F关系交流；再补B健康精力、D金钱选择、G社会环境；最后熟悉七个故事的可替换细节。

PART 01

七条结果链｜14个旧论点压缩到7个

每题只选一条。方括号内换成题目内容；不要在一道题里叠加两条完整结果链。

A

减压、专注与避免打扰

★★★★★

安静环境、独处、音乐、数码休息、压力、专注

It creates a calmer and more predictable environment. It helps me reduce stress and feel calmer and more in control. By reducing [STRESS, NOISE, OR DIGITAL DISTRACTIONS], it allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I concentrate fully, I understand the task better and make fewer careless mistakes. As a result, I can study or work with greater control and confidence.

B

健康、精力与稳定状态

★★★★★

运动、饮食、睡眠、户外活动、规律作息

A regular [EXERCISE, MEAL, OR SLEEP] routine supports both my physical health and daily energy. It helps my body recover and prevents sudden tiredness or hunger from interrupting my work. With steadier energy, I can remain focused during classes and complete tasks more consistently. Over time, the habit makes a healthy lifestyle easier to maintain.

C

节省时间与便利

★★★★★

网购、科技、远程工作、日程安排、家务、交通

It removes unnecessary [TRAVEL, WAITING, OR REPEATED DECISIONS] from my routine. This allows me to finish essential tasks more efficiently and deal with unexpected changes more easily. The time I save can be used for studying, relaxing, or spending time with people who matter to me. As a result, my schedule becomes more flexible without becoming less productive.

D

节省金钱与理性选择

★★★★★

消费、预算、购物、职业选择、信息可靠性

It gives me enough information to compare [PRICE, QUALITY, OR LONG-TERM VALUE] before making a decision. It raises my awareness of the importance of saving money. It also helps me avoid unnecessary spending and use my money more wisely. I can choose the option that fits both my actual needs and my budget. Over time, these small decisions help me use money more wisely and avoid unnecessary waste.

E

成长、技能与自信

★★★★★

目标、练习、反馈、教育、阅读、新技能、独立

Setting a clear goal turns a difficult task into manageable steps. Regular practice or useful feedback shows me what I need to improve and what I should do next. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities. Over time, I can apply what I have learned and handle unfamiliar challenges more independently.

F

关系、交流与拓宽眼界

★★★★★

亲友、合作、旅行、节日、历史、文化、不同观点

It gives people a good chance to spend quality time together and exchange real experiences. Through conversation or cooperation, they can understand one another’s needs and see the same issue from different perspectives. This builds trust while making them more open-minded in unfamiliar situations. Over time, the shared experience creates a stronger and more meaningful connection.

G

社会、环境与公共影响

★★★★★

环保、能源、回收、公共交通、公园、社区服务、公平

It benefits more than one person by improving [PUBLIC ACCESS, THE LOCAL ENVIRONMENT, OR A SHARED RESOURCE]. More specifically, [ACTION] helps people reduce [POLLUTION, COST, OR A COMMON RISK] in a practical way. The effect becomes stronger when residents repeat the action or participate together. As a result, the community becomes more accessible, responsible, and sustainable over time.

PART 02

七个故事世界｜12个旧例子压缩到7个

经历题直接选一个故事并替换方括号；观点题只借用其中一条短细节，不要背完整故事。

A

户外散步＋拍照

户外、自然、园艺、天气、公园、运动、摄影、减压

Last month, I spent a Saturday on [A FOREST TRAIL OR IN A BOTANICAL GARDEN] with two friends. We walked for three hours, took photos of [THE LAKE OR CHANGES IN THE LIGHT], and talked without checking our phones. Because rain was forecast, we carried small umbrellas and left early. The activity gave me gentle exercise as well as a calm break from studying. As a result, I returned to campus feeling relaxed, observant, and focused.

气候题只换成：My city has hot, humid summers, mild winters, and frequent rain in spring.

B

钢琴学习｜活动、困难、反馈共用

爱好、音乐、艺术、目标、新技能、反馈、儿童教育、减压

I practice the piano for about twenty minutes several evenings a week. A familiar piece helps me slow down after class, while a difficult section gives me a clear goal. The main difficulty was an awkward hand position, so my teacher showed me a simpler movement and asked me to practice slowly. Piano music can express delicate feelings that are difficult to put into words. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities.

C

聪明购物｜一个故事换商品

网购、实体店、预算、个人理财、食物、饮食、服装、礼物

Last Saturday, I bought [NOTEBOOKS ONLINE, RUNNING SHOES IN A STORE, GROCERIES, A REFERENCE BOOK, OR A GIFT]. Before paying, I compared [PRICES, SIZES, INGREDIENTS, OR TWO EDITIONS] instead of making a rushed decision. For an item involving fit, I tested it in person; for a routine product, I ordered it online in a few minutes. This helped me choose what I actually needed, avoid a return, and stay within my budget. As a result, the purchase was both practical and cost-effective.

饮食题使用同一购物经历：I bought vegetables, eggs, yogurt, whole grains, and chicken, and prepared two simple meals for the week.

D

学生公寓｜作息、睡眠与家务共用

居住、睡眠、日常安排、时间管理、家务、装饰、数码休息、远程工作

I live with one roommate in a small apartment near campus. Each evening, I put my phone on the desk, prepare my clothes and task list for the next morning, and listen to quiet piano music for ten minutes. Last month, we moved a shelf and added labeled storage boxes, which made cleaning faster and the room more comfortable for studying. When my plans change or I sleep somewhere unfamiliar, I keep the same short routine and carry earplugs. This helps me remain calm, organized, and focused.

E

课程＋小组项目

课程、阅读、教育、科技、团队、在线学习、反馈

Last semester, three classmates and I prepared a course project about [BIOLOGY, TECHNOLOGY, OR CAMPUS LIFE]. We used a short online lesson to understand the topic, divided the research and speaking tasks, and held brief check-ins. The main difficulty was an unreliable source, so each teammate verified one piece of information before we used it. Our teacher’s feedback helped us reorganize one confusing section. As a result, we finished on time and produced a clearer presentation together.

F

家人＋文化出行

家人、朋友、旅行、节日、历史、文化、传统、眼界

During the last holiday, I visited [A HISTORY MUSEUM OR A CULTURAL FESTIVAL] with my grandfather. We examined old photographs, watched a local performance, and talked about how the community had changed. He connected one display to a story from his childhood, which made the information more personal and memorable. The visit showed me a perspective I could not get from a short textbook description. As a result, I understood the tradition better and felt closer to my grandfather.

G

图书馆＋社区服务

工作、社区服务、环保、回收、公共资源、沟通、责任

Last semester, I volunteered at a neighborhood library during a community event about [RECYCLING, PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION, OR ENERGY SAVING]. I organized materials, helped visitors find reliable information, and explained one simple action they could try at home. When a visitor asked a question I could not answer, I checked the library database instead of guessing. By the end of the event, several families had joined the program. The experience improved my communication skills and showed me how small actions can create a wider public benefit.

少数必须单独替换的最短细节：

服装：I prefer simple, comfortable clothes in neutral colors. A plain T-shirt, loose trousers, and supportive shoes are easy to match and suitable for both class and walking.

电影：I recently watched a documentary about a young climber who recovered from an injury and returned to competition. I liked it because the story showed how patience and small goals can rebuild confidence.

工作：My friend works part time as a library assistant. She organizes returned books, helps students locate reliable sources, and updates borrowing records.

在线考试：Online test results are more trustworthy when schools verify students’ identities, randomize questions, and use short oral follow-ups.

社交媒体：It is appropriate to share study resources, public event information, and landscape photos, but not another person’s private information or location without permission.

公共空间：Keep basic access free and charge only for optional services; where housing is seriously limited, prioritize housing while preserving smaller parks near residential buildings.

PART 03

四种答题结构｜覆盖所有提问方式

这四套只负责组织答案，不是额外论点。一个答案只能选择一种结构。

1

事实、习惯与过去经历

★★★★★

频率、种类、日常做法、人物地点、最近或难忘的经历

[Usually, I / Last month, I] [DIRECT ANSWER]. [I normally / On that occasion, I] [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. The most important detail [is / was] [DETAIL], because [REASON]. As a result, [OBSERVABLE OUTCOME].

2

面对困难与解决问题

★★★★★

睡眠、天气、学习、园艺、回收、工作、考试可靠性；讲过去经历时，只需把 is/can/make/deal 改为 was/could/made/dealt

The main difficulty is [PROBLEM], because it can [NEGATIVE RESULT]. A practical way to handle it is to [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. For example, [SHORT OBSERVABLE DETAIL]. These steps make the situation more predictable and reduce the risk of [RISK]. As a result, [PERSON OR GROUP] can deal with the problem more calmly and effectively.

3

观点、选择与双方取舍

★★★★★

同意或不同意、A/B选择、重要性、是否应该、双方都有价值

I [AGREE / DISAGREE / WOULD CHOOSE A], mainly because [REASON]. In practical terms, [MECHANISM]. For example, [SHORT OBSERVABLE DETAIL]. As a result, [RESULT]. If the question presents two reasonable sides, add: [OPTION B] remains useful when [CONDITION], but [OPTION A] better fits [MY NEED OR THE MORE URGENT NEED].

4

未来变化与是否会被取代

★★★★

增加、减少、继续存在、成为趋势、传统方式是否消失

I expect [TREND] to [INCREASE, DECREASE, OR CONTINUE], mainly because [DRIVER]. As [CHANGE], [GROUP] will be able to [BENEFIT]. For example, [CURRENT SIGNAL]. However, [OLDER OR ALTERNATIVE OPTION] will remain valuable when [CONDITION]. Therefore, the future will probably involve a gradual shift rather than complete replacement.

组合规则：

结构1：直接使用一个故事世界，不再接整条结果链。

结构2：填入两项具体行动和结果，不再讲另一个完整故事。

结构3：立场句＋一条结果链中最相关的两句＋一条短细节。

结构4：趋势骨架＋一条结果链中最相关的两句。目标是约75—90词，而不是越长越好。
