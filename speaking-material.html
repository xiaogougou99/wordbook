TOEFL SPEAKING

口语通用表达与高复用例子

先背“作用点”，再背四个共用补缺骨架，最后掌握少量高复用例子。答题时只选择一个作用点或骨架，接一个可观察细节和结果，不要把多个整段硬塞进同一道题。

建议顺序：先背减压、专注、成长、眼界、关系、健康、时间和金钱；再背四个共用骨架；最后用后面的例子练习替换细节。

PART 01

通用作用点｜每段四句，可直接背

A

缓解压力

★★★★★

学习、工作、运动、音乐、散步、休闲活动

It helps me temporarily forget about my assignments and just enjoy the moment. For a short time, I can step away from academic pressure and give my mind a real break. Afterward, I feel much calmer and less overwhelmed. As a result, I can focus better and get my work done more efficiently.

B

避免被打扰与保持专注

★★★★★

学习、工作、阅读、音乐、独处、安静环境

It helps me stay focused and makes me less likely to get distracted by things happening around me. When I can concentrate on one thing at a time, I usually understand it better and work more efficiently. It also keeps me from switching to unrelated activities, so I can use my time in a more organized way. As a result, I can get more things done and feel more productive throughout the day.

C

★★★★★

个人目标、学习计划、新技能、习惯、自信

Setting clear goals gives me a sense of direction and keeps me motivated. It helps me understand what I need to improve and what steps I should take next. As I make progress, I become more confident in my abilities. Over time, this process helps me grow and become a better version of myself.

D

拓宽眼界

★★★★★

新技能、课程、文化、旅行、阅读、交流

Learning [SKILL OR SUBJECT] exposes me to new ideas and different ways of thinking. More importantly, it broadens my horizons by showing me how other people approach the same problem. This helps me look at things from different perspectives instead of sticking to just one way of thinking. Over time, this wider perspective makes me more open-minded, adaptable, and confident in unfamiliar situations.

E

家人、朋友、同学、共同活动、旅行、聚餐

It gives me a good chance to spend more quality time with other people. We can talk, share our experiences, and understand each other better. This makes our relationship closer and helps build more trust between us. Over time, these shared experiences can create a stronger and more meaningful connection.

F

With more energy, I can focus better on my studies or work and get more things done. That is why I think it is a practical way to improve both my health and my daily performance.

G

避免浪费时间

★★★★★

网购、计划、清单、科技、交通、学习方法

It helps me avoid wasting time on unnecessary things. I can focus on what really matters and finish my tasks more efficiently. This also gives me more free time for studying, relaxing, or doing something I enjoy. That is why I think it is a much better use of my time.

H

★★★★★

购物、预算、比价、二手物品、消费选择

It helps me avoid spending money on things I do not really need. I can compare different options, make better choices, and use my money more wisely. Over time, this can help me save a considerable amount of money. That is why I think it is a more practical and cost-effective choice.

I

实践与长期记忆

★★★★

实习、社团、旅行、实验、实地参观、新技能

It gives me a chance to apply what I have learned in a real situation. By actually doing the task, I can understand how the idea works instead of simply memorizing it. The experience also makes the information more vivid and easier to remember. Over time, repeated practice helps me turn abstract knowledge into a useful skill.

J

获取信息与做出更好选择

★★★★

It gives me access to useful information from several different sources. I can compare the available options and understand the possible results before making a decision. This keeps me from acting too quickly or relying on incomplete assumptions. As a result, I can make a more informed choice that fits my actual needs.

Other people can notice problems that I may not see by myself. Their feedback helps me identify a weakness early and adjust my approach before the same mistake becomes a habit. I still need to put in my own effort, but the guidance makes that effort more focused and efficient. Over time, this combination of personal practice and outside support helps me improve more steadily.

I can then use that information to improve my next decision. Over time, this process makes me more creative, flexible, and confident when I face an unfamiliar problem.

PART 02

补缺只背四套｜相似题统一说法

这四套不是额外观点，而是把“描述、困难、取舍、趋势”统一成固定的展开方式。方括号内容必须根据题目替换。

1

描述、习惯与流程

★★★★★

气候、衣着、饮食、作息、居住、购物、工作内容

I would describe [THING OR ROUTINE] as [FEATURE 1] and [FEATURE 2]. More specifically, I usually [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. This works well for me because [REASON]. As a result, [OBSERVABLE OUTCOME].

2

面对困难与解决问题

★★★★★

睡眠、天气、学习、园艺、回收、工作、考试可靠性

The main difficulty is [PROBLEM], because it can [NEGATIVE RESULT]. A practical way to handle it is to [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. These steps make the situation more predictable and reduce the risk of [RISK]. As a result, [PERSON OR GROUP] can deal with the problem more calmly and effectively.

3

两项都有价值时的取舍

★★★★★

免费或收费、公园或住房、严格或灵活、线上或线下、个人或政府

Both options have value, but I would prioritize [OPTION A] because it addresses the more immediate and widespread need. [OPTION A] directly [MAIN BENEFIT], while [OPTION B] is especially useful when [CONDITION]. A practical compromise is to [BALANCED SOLUTION]. This protects [CORE VALUE] without ignoring [SECOND VALUE].

4

未来会增长，但不会完全取代

★★★★

网购、远程工作、线上学习、金融软件、电影院、科技趋势

I expect [NEW TREND] to become more common, mainly because [DRIVER]. As [CHANGE], more people will be able to [BENEFIT]. However, [TRADITIONAL OPTION] will remain valuable when [CONDITION]. Therefore, the future will probably involve a gradual shift rather than complete replacement.

PART 03

高复用例子｜接在作用点后面

临场只需更换人物、时间、地点或物品。每个例子都能连接前面的多个作用点。

A

徒步＋朋友＋摄影

减压、关系、健康、艺术表达、户外活动

Last month, I spent a Saturday on a forest trail with two friends. We walked for three hours, took photos of the lake, and talked without checking our phones. Taking pictures made me notice small changes in light and color, so the activity was both physical and creative. I returned to campus feeling calmer, more focused, and closer to my friends.

B

钢琴＋音乐＋艺术表达

减压、专注、目标、成长、艺术、新技能

I practice the piano for about twenty minutes several evenings a week. A familiar piece helps me slow down after class, while a difficult section gives me a clear goal to work toward. Piano music can capture delicate inner feelings and express a personal perspective without words. When I can finally play a section smoothly, the visible progress gives me both confidence and a healthy way to express emotion.

C

网购＋课程表＋预算

节省时间、节省金钱、科技、时间管理、日常习惯

I shop online for routine items because it fits my class schedule and student budget. For example, I can reorder notebooks in five minutes and compare several prices before paying. This saves the travel and waiting time required by a physical store and keeps me from making a rushed purchase. As a result, I can spend more time studying and stay within my monthly budget.

D

实体店＋试穿＋当场确认

购物偏好、可靠选择、避免退货、预算、服务

I prefer visiting a physical store when size or comfort matters. I recently tried on three pairs of running shoes and noticed that only one supported my feet comfortably. A staff member also adjusted the laces and explained the return policy before I paid. As a result, I avoided a sizing mistake and chose a product I could use immediately.

E

老师反馈＋钢琴纠错

接受帮助、反馈、学习技能、提高效率、个人成长

While learning the piano, I once kept using an awkward hand position without noticing it. My teacher showed me a simpler movement and asked me to practice the same passage slowly. After several sessions, I could play it more smoothly and with fewer mistakes. I still did the practice myself, but the feedback prevented me from repeating the wrong movement.

F

博物馆＋文化传承

文化、历史、拓宽眼界、实践记忆、家庭关系

Last winter, I visited a local history museum with my grandfather. He explained how several old tools were used in his childhood, which made the objects feel more personal than the descriptions in my textbook. I began to understand how daily life in the region had changed across generations. The visit helped me remember the history and gave us a meaningful topic to discuss afterward.

G

小组项目＋明确分工

合作、沟通、信任、效率、解决困难

Last semester, three classmates and I prepared a short presentation for a biology course. We divided the research, slides, and speaking parts according to each person’s strengths and held one brief check-in every two days. When one source was unreliable, a teammate noticed the problem and replaced it before the deadline. As a result, we finished on time and gave a clearer presentation than any of us could have produced alone.

H

在线课程＋实际练习

信息、时间、技能、实践、拓宽眼界

I recently used a short online course to learn basic photo editing. I watched one lesson after class and immediately applied the technique to a picture I had taken near the campus lake. Comparing the original image with the edited version showed me exactly what the new tool could do. That visible result made the lesson easier to remember and encouraged me to continue learning.

I

学生公寓＋睡前习惯＋整理房间

居住情况、睡前流程、陌生环境、家务、房间装饰、专注

I live with one roommate in a small apartment near campus. Before bed, I put my phone on the desk, prepare my clothes for the next morning, and listen to quiet piano music for ten minutes. Last month, we moved a shelf away from the window and added labeled storage boxes, which made the room easier to clean and more comfortable for studying. When I stay somewhere unfamiliar, I follow the same routine and bring earplugs, so I can relax and fall asleep more easily.

J

健康饮食＋买菜＋外出就餐

饮食计划、常吃食物、杂货购物、菜系偏好、健康、预算

I follow a simple balanced eating plan rather than a strict diet. On weekends, I buy vegetables, eggs, yogurt, whole grains, and chicken, and then prepare two basic meals for the week. These choices give me steady energy and keep me from making rushed purchases of fast food. When I eat out, I usually choose light Asian dishes such as vegetable soup or grilled chicken because they are familiar, nutritious, and not overly oily.

K

多雨城市＋个人准备＋公共措施

当地气候、下雨准备、极端天气、政府措施、解决困难

My city has hot, humid summers, mild winters, and frequent rain in spring. When rain is forecast, I carry a small umbrella, wear waterproof shoes, and leave home ten minutes early. At the community level, accurate alerts and well-maintained drainage systems give residents time to prepare and reduce the risk of flooding. These steps make unpredictable weather safer and less disruptive.

L

植物园＋摄影＋解决种植困难

园艺、植物、自然、摄影、减压、获取信息

Last spring, I visited a botanical garden with a friend and took photos of several colorful plants. The herb garden was especially appealing because each plant had a label explaining how it was used. A staff member said that sunlight, watering schedules, and pests were the main challenges, so beginners should start with hardy herbs. The visit taught me something practical and gave me a calm break from studying.

少数必须单独替换的最短细节：

服装：I prefer simple, comfortable clothes in neutral colors. A plain T-shirt, loose trousers, and supportive shoes are easy to match and suitable for both class and walking.

电影：I recently watched a documentary about a young climber who recovered from an injury and returned to competition. I liked it because the story showed how patience and small goals can rebuild confidence.

工作：My friend works part time as a library assistant. She organizes returned books, helps students locate reliable sources, and updates borrowing records.

在线考试：Online test results are more trustworthy when schools verify students’ identities, randomize questions, and use short oral follow-ups.

社交媒体：It is appropriate to share study resources, public event information, and landscape photos, but not another person’s private information or location without permission.

公共空间：Keep basic access free and charge only for optional services; where housing is seriously limited, prioritize housing while preserving smaller parks near residential buildings.
