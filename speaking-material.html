TOEFL SPEAKING

口语通用表达与高复用例子

先背四组“母句”，再记每个作用点不同的开头；遇到描述、困难、取舍或趋势题时，再调用四个共用骨架。最后只需接一个高复用例子，不要把多个整段硬塞进同一道题。

最省背诵量的顺序：先背专注、进步、选择、视角四组母句；再学习每张卡片如何接入母句；最后用后面的例子练习替换细节。

PART 01

通用作用点｜每段四句，可直接背

同一组里的后两句故意完全相同。临场只需根据题目更换前面的原因，后面的逻辑和语言不变。

只背四组母句：

① 专注链（A/B/F/G）：This allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I do that, I understand the task better and use my time more efficiently.

② 进步链（C/I/K/L）：It helps me see what I need to improve and what step I should take next. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities.

③ 选择链（H/J）：I can compare the available options before making a decision. As a result, I can make a more informed choice that fits my actual needs.

④ 视角链（D/N）：Learning about [SUBJECT] exposes me to ideas and experiences beyond my daily life. This helps me look at things from different perspectives instead of sticking to one way of thinking.

A

缓解压力

★★★★★

学习、工作、运动、音乐、散步、休闲活动

It helps me step away from academic pressure and gives my mind a real break. Afterward, I feel calmer and less overwhelmed. This allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I do that, I understand the task better and use my time more efficiently.

B

避免被打扰与保持专注

★★★★★

学习、工作、阅读、音乐、独处、安静环境

It reduces the distractions around me and keeps me from switching to unrelated activities. This makes my work more organized and easier to manage. This allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I do that, I understand the task better and use my time more efficiently.

C

设置目标与个人成长

★★★★★

个人目标、学习计划、新技能、习惯、自信

Setting clear goals gives me a sense of direction and keeps me motivated. It helps me see what I need to improve and what step I should take next. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities. Over time, this process helps me grow and handle new challenges more independently.

D

拓宽眼界

★★★★★

新技能、课程、文化、旅行、阅读、交流

Learning about [SKILL OR SUBJECT] exposes me to ideas and experiences beyond my daily life. This helps me look at things from different perspectives instead of sticking to one way of thinking. I can then adapt what I learn to an unfamiliar situation. Over time, this wider perspective makes me more open-minded, adaptable, and confident.

E

增进关系

★★★★★

家人、朋友、同学、共同活动、旅行、聚餐

It gives me a good chance to spend more quality time with other people. We can talk, share our experiences, and understand each other better. This makes our relationship closer and helps build more trust between us. Over time, these shared experiences can create a stronger and more meaningful connection.

F

★★★★★

It helps me stay healthy and gives me more energy throughout the day. With enough energy, I feel more active and less tired when I study or work. This allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I do that, I understand the task better and use my time more efficiently.

G

避免浪费时间

★★★★★

网购、计划、清单、科技、交通、学习方法

It helps me avoid wasting time on unnecessary things. I can complete the essential steps first and leave more time for studying, relaxing, or doing something I enjoy. This allows me to focus on one thing at a time. When I do that, I understand the task better and use my time more efficiently.

H

★★★★★

It helps me avoid spending money on things I do not really need. I can compare the available options before making a decision. This keeps me from making a rushed purchase and helps me stay within my budget. As a result, I can make a more informed choice that fits my actual needs.

I

★★★★

实习、社团、旅行、实验、实地参观、新技能

It gives me a chance to apply what I have learned in a real situation. By actually doing the task, I understand the idea more clearly and remember it more easily. It helps me see what I need to improve and what step I should take next. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities.

J

It gives me access to useful information from several different sources. I can compare the available options before making a decision. This keeps me from acting too quickly or relying on incomplete assumptions. As a result, I can make a more informed choice that fits my actual needs.

Over time, I become more willing to understand and preserve that part of the community.

PART 02

补缺只背四套｜相似题统一说法

这四套不是额外观点，而是把“描述、困难、取舍、趋势”统一成固定的展开方式。方括号内容必须根据题目替换。

1

描述、习惯与流程

★★★★★

气候、衣着、饮食、作息、居住、购物、工作内容

I would describe [THING OR ROUTINE] as [FEATURE 1] and [FEATURE 2]. More specifically, I usually [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. This works well for me because [REASON]. As a result, [OBSERVABLE OUTCOME].

2

面对困难与解决问题

★★★★★

睡眠、天气、学习、园艺、回收、工作、考试可靠性；讲过去经历时，只需把 is/can/make/deal 改为 was/could/made/dealt

The main difficulty is [PROBLEM], because it can [NEGATIVE RESULT]. A practical way to handle it is to [ACTION 1] and [ACTION 2]. These steps make the situation more predictable and reduce the risk of [RISK]. As a result, [PERSON OR GROUP] can deal with the problem more calmly and effectively.

3

两项都有价值时的取舍

★★★★★

免费或收费、公园或住房、严格或灵活、线上或线下、个人或政府

Both options have value, but I would prioritize [OPTION A] because it addresses the more immediate and widespread need. [OPTION A] directly [MAIN BENEFIT], while [OPTION B] is especially useful when [CONDITION]. A practical compromise is to [BALANCED SOLUTION]. This protects [CORE VALUE] without ignoring [SECOND VALUE].

4

未来会增长，但不会完全取代

★★★★

网购、远程工作、线上学习、金融软件、电影院、科技趋势

I expect [NEW TREND] to become more common, mainly because [DRIVER]. As [CHANGE], more people will be able to [BENEFIT]. However, [TRADITIONAL OPTION] will remain valuable when [CONDITION]. Therefore, the future will probably involve a gradual shift rather than complete replacement.

PART 03

高复用例子｜接在作用点后面

临场只需更换人物、时间、地点或物品。每个例子都能连接前面的多个作用点。

A

徒步＋朋友＋摄影

减压、关系、健康、艺术表达、户外活动

Last month, I spent a Saturday on a forest trail with two friends. We walked for three hours, took photos of the lake, and talked without checking our phones. Taking pictures made me notice small changes in light and color, so the activity was both physical and creative. I returned to campus feeling calmer, more focused, and closer to my friends.

B

钢琴＋音乐＋艺术表达

减压、专注、目标、成长、艺术、新技能

I practice the piano for about twenty minutes several evenings a week. A familiar piece helps me slow down after class, while a difficult section gives me a clear goal to work toward. Piano music can capture delicate inner feelings and express a personal perspective without words. As I make visible progress, I become more confident in my abilities.

C

网购＋课程表＋预算

节省时间、节省金钱、科技、时间管理、日常习惯

I shop online for routine items because it fits my class schedule and student budget. For example, I can reorder notebooks in five minutes and compare several prices before paying. This saves the travel and waiting time required by a physical store and keeps me from making a rushed purchase. As a result, I can spend more time studying and stay within my monthly budget.

D

实体店＋试穿＋当场确认

购物偏好、可靠选择、避免退货、预算、服务

I prefer visiting a physical store when size or comfort matters. I recently tried on three pairs of running shoes and noticed that only one supported my feet comfortably. A staff member also adjusted the laces and explained the return policy before I paid. As a result, I avoided a sizing mistake and chose a product I could use immediately.

E

老师反馈＋钢琴纠错

接受帮助、反馈、学习技能、提高效率、个人成长

The main difficulty was an awkward hand position, because it made me repeat the same mistake. A practical way to handle it was to follow my teacher’s demonstration and practice the same passage slowly. These steps made the movement more predictable and reduced unnecessary mistakes. As I made visible progress, I became more confident in my abilities.

F

博物馆＋文化传承

文化、历史、拓宽眼界、实践记忆、家庭关系

Last winter, I visited a local history museum with my grandfather. He explained how several old tools were used in his childhood, which made the objects feel more personal than the descriptions in my textbook. I began to understand how daily life in the region had changed across generations. The visit helped me remember the history and gave us a meaningful topic to discuss afterward.

G

小组项目＋明确分工

合作、沟通、信任、效率、解决困难

Last semester, three classmates and I prepared a short presentation for a biology course. The main difficulty was an unreliable source, because it could weaken the whole presentation. A practical way to handle it was to divide the work clearly and ask each teammate to verify one source during our brief check-ins. As a result, we finished on time and produced a clearer presentation together.

H

在线课程＋实际练习

信息、时间、技能、实践、拓宽眼界

I recently used a short online course to learn basic photo editing. I watched one lesson after class and immediately applied the technique to a picture I had taken near the campus lake. Comparing the original image with the edited version showed me what I needed to improve next. As I made visible progress, I became more confident in my abilities.

I

学生公寓＋睡前习惯＋整理房间

居住情况、睡前流程、陌生环境、家务、房间装饰、专注

I live with one roommate in a small apartment near campus. Before bed, I put my phone on the desk, prepare my clothes for the next morning, and listen to quiet piano music for ten minutes. Last month, we moved a shelf away from the window and added labeled storage boxes, which made the room easier to clean and more comfortable for studying. When I stay somewhere unfamiliar, I follow the same routine and bring earplugs, so I can relax and fall asleep more easily.

J

健康饮食＋买菜＋外出就餐

饮食计划、常吃食物、杂货购物、菜系偏好、健康、预算

I follow a simple balanced eating plan rather than a strict diet. On weekends, I buy vegetables, eggs, yogurt, whole grains, and chicken, and then prepare two basic meals for the week. These choices give me steady energy and keep me from making rushed purchases of fast food. When I eat out, I usually choose light Asian dishes such as vegetable soup or grilled chicken because they are familiar, nutritious, and not overly oily.

K

多雨城市＋个人准备＋公共措施

当地气候、下雨准备、极端天气、政府措施、解决困难

My city has hot, humid summers, mild winters, and frequent rain in spring. The main difficulty is sudden rain, because it can delay trips and increase the risk of flooding. A practical way to handle it is to carry an umbrella and leave early, while the city sends accurate alerts and maintains drainage systems. As a result, residents can deal with the weather more calmly and effectively.

L

植物园＋摄影＋解决种植困难

园艺、植物、自然、摄影、减压、获取信息

Last spring, I visited a botanical garden with a friend and took photos of several colorful plants. The main difficulty for beginners is keeping plants healthy, because too much water or too little sunlight can damage them. A practical way to handle it is to start with hardy herbs and follow a simple watering schedule. The visit taught me something practical and gave me a calm break from studying.

少数必须单独替换的最短细节：

服装：I prefer simple, comfortable clothes in neutral colors. A plain T-shirt, loose trousers, and supportive shoes are easy to match and suitable for both class and walking.

电影：I recently watched a documentary about a young climber who recovered from an injury and returned to competition. I liked it because the story showed how patience and small goals can rebuild confidence.

工作：My friend works part time as a library assistant. She organizes returned books, helps students locate reliable sources, and updates borrowing records.

在线考试：Online test results are more trustworthy when schools verify students’ identities, randomize questions, and use short oral follow-ups.

社交媒体：It is appropriate to share study resources, public event information, and landscape photos, but not another person’s private information or location without permission.

公共空间：Keep basic access free and charge only for optional services; where housing is seriously limited, prioritize housing while preserving smaller parks near residential buildings.
