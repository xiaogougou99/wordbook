PART 01

最高优先级｜跨题材主链

A

提高效率与分配资源

★★★★★

科技、教育、交通、管理、公共服务、线上平台

减少低价值步骤 → 释放资源 → 投入重要任务 → 效率提高

One major advantage of [CHOICE] is its ability to reduce the time and effort required to [GOAL]. When [MECHANISM], people can redirect limited resources away from [LOW-VALUE TASK] and toward [HIGHER-VALUE ACTIVITY]. This reallocation not only improves immediate efficiency but also makes high-quality results easier to sustain under practical constraints. Therefore, [CHOICE] is particularly valuable when time, funding, or staff are limited.

B

实践、技能与成长

★★★★★

课程、实习、兼职、社团、旅行、职业培训

实际行动 → 应用知识 → 掌握技能 → 获得信心

[ACTIVITY] enables people to translate abstract knowledge into practical competence through repeated exposure to real-world situations. By [ACTION], they learn to [SKILL], receive concrete feedback, and refine their approach accordingly. As their competence becomes visible, they gain confidence and become more willing to take on unfamiliar challenges. Over time, this self-reinforcing cycle of application, feedback, and adjustment produces lasting improvement.

C

兴趣、动力与正向循环

★★★★★

教育方法、职业选择、员工培训、儿童学习、社团

兴趣 → 持续参与 → 练习增加 → 看见进步 → 动力增强

When [CHOICE] aligns with people’s interests, they are more likely to sustain their engagement and recognize a meaningful connection between effort and results. Regular participation creates more opportunities for deliberate practice, while visible progress reinforces their sense of competence. That growing confidence, in turn, motivates them to persist when difficulties arise. Consequently, [CHOICE] is more likely to produce durable improvement than an approach driven primarily by external pressure.

D

反馈、合作与提高质量

★★★★★

教师指导、团队合作、同伴学习、专家评价、培训

不同视角 → 发现盲点 → 及时纠错 → 质量与效率提高

Working with [PEOPLE] exposes individuals to perspectives and problems they might not recognize independently. Timely, specific feedback enables them to diagnose weaknesses early and refine their approach before minor errors become entrenched. As a result, they produce higher-quality work while avoiding unnecessary revision. This is particularly valuable when a task is complex and sound judgment depends on examining it from more than one angle.

E

前期投入与长期稳定

★★★★★

教育投资、环境、基础设施、培训、健康、安全

前期投入 → 解决根源 → 避免反复 → 减少未来损失

Although [CHOICE] may require substantial time or money at the outset, it can prevent the same problem from recurring. By identifying [RISK] early and addressing its underlying cause, [GROUP] can avoid more serious damage and escalating costs later. The initial expenditure should therefore be understood as a preventive investment rather than an immediate loss. In the long run, it creates greater stability and more durable benefits.

F

信息、判断与避免错误

★★★★★

互联网、新闻、广告、职业、消费、公共决策

可靠信息 → 比较选择 → 预判结果 → 减少错误

Access to reliable information enables people to compare alternatives and anticipate the likely consequences of each choice. Rather than relying on habit or incomplete assumptions, they can select the option that best aligns with their needs and constraints. This reduces avoidable errors and makes their decisions more transparent and defensible. Over time, consistently informed choices can improve both individual outcomes and public trust.

PART 02

第二优先级｜题材匹配时调用

G

可及性、公平与机会

★★★★

网络教育、交通、补贴、医疗、住房、公共设施

降低门槛 → 获得资源 → 改善能力 → 缩小机会差距

Making [RESOURCE] more accessible enables people constrained by time, income, or location to pursue opportunities that would otherwise remain beyond their reach. Once access is secured, they can develop [SKILL] or receive [SERVICE], strengthening their capacity to participate in education, work, and community life. Broader participation can gradually narrow persistent opportunity gaps. Consequently, improved access promotes both individual independence and wider social mobility.

H

健康、专注与持续表现

★★★★

饮食、运动、休闲、绿地、作息、屏幕使用

压力减少 → 注意力提高 → 错误减少 → 避免长期倦怠

[HABIT OR POLICY] can alleviate stress and make a healthy routine easier to sustain. When people are adequately rested and emotionally balanced, they can maintain concentration for longer periods, commit fewer errors, and respond to setbacks more calmly. These immediate gains also lower the long-term risk of exhaustion and burnout. Supporting well-being therefore improves not only health but also the consistency of performance.

I

沟通、信任与合作

★★★★

家庭、学校、社团、团队、社区、公司

交流 → 理解需求 → 建立信任 → 合作效率提高

Regular interaction creates opportunities for people to exchange information, recognize one another’s needs, and resolve misunderstandings before they escalate. As mutual trust develops, participants become more willing to share responsibility and coordinate on demanding tasks. This reduces conflict and improves the efficiency of collective action. Over time, [ACTIVITY OR POLICY] can strengthen both personal relationships and the effectiveness of the wider group.

J

创新、试错与新方案

★★★★

AI、科技、企业冒险、改革、创业、研究

尝试新方法 → 暴露问题 → 根据反馈改进 → 形成新方案

Carefully controlled experimentation allows individuals and organizations to test ideas that conventional methods may overlook. Even an unsuccessful early attempt can generate useful evidence about which assumptions or procedures require revision. Applying that evidence in subsequent trials leads to increasingly sound decisions and more effective solutions. Strategic risk-taking can therefore produce meaningful innovation, provided that potential harm is limited and outcomes are evaluated rigorously.

K

环保行为的累积效果

★★★

公共交通、回收、能源、企业政策、消费选择

单次影响小 → 大量人重复 → 效果累积 → 形成更多习惯

A single action may appear insignificant, yet its cumulative effect can become substantial when many people repeat it over time. [ACTION] directly reduces [WASTE, EMISSIONS, OR RESOURCE USE] and also makes participants more conscious of the environmental consequences of everyday choices. Greater awareness can, in turn, encourage additional responsible habits. Consequently, a simple policy can generate both immediate resource savings and durable environmental benefits.

L

文化、历史与身份认同

★★★

历史建筑、博物馆、艺术、传统文化、旅游

接触文化实体 → 理解历史 → 身份认同 → 教育和旅游价值

Historical sites and cultural programs provide a tangible connection to the experiences and values of earlier generations. Encountering [OBJECT OR PRACTICE] in its original context allows visitors to understand the past more vividly than abstract descriptions alone would permit. Such engagement strengthens cultural identity while also supporting education and local tourism. Preservation, however, should prioritize places and practices with demonstrable educational, cultural, or economic value.

M

制度、规模与累积影响

★★★★

历史力量、政府政策、经济制度、社会规范、全球化、城市发展

共同条件 → 大规模影响行为 → 跨群体累积 → 形成持久改变

[SYSTEMIC FORCE] shapes the choices available to large groups of people at the same time. When [MECHANISM] repeatedly influences everyday behavior, its effects accumulate across communities and generations. Individual leaders may accelerate or redirect this process, but lasting change usually depends on whether the wider system enables millions of people to act in similar ways. Therefore, system-level forces often produce broader and more durable change than isolated individual actions.

7月真题补缺｜Historical Forces

题目：What do you believe has a greater influence on shaping history: individual actions or broader social and economic forces? Why?

I agree with Claire that broader social and economic forces usually shape history more profoundly than individual actions. Such forces influence the choices available to entire populations at the same time. When economic incentives, institutions, and new technologies repeatedly alter how people work, travel, and communicate, their effects accumulate across communities and generations. For example, the Industrial Revolution transformed production not because of one leader’s decision, but because technological advances, investment, and urban labor expanded together. Individual leaders can certainly accelerate or redirect these changes, as Andrew suggests, yet their influence rarely lasts unless wider conditions allow millions of people to respond in similar ways. Therefore, leaders often determine the timing of change, whereas systemic forces determine its scale and durability.

PART 03

三个论证发动机｜必须背

1

因果链

★★★★★

The main reason is that [CAUSE]. When [CONDITION], [MECHANISM], which means [IMMEDIATE RESULT]. Over time, this can [LARGER CONSEQUENCE].

2

例证链

★★★★★

For example, [PERSON, SCHOOL, COMPANY, OR CITY] recently [ACTION]. Because [RELEVANT DETAIL], [OBSERVABLE RESULT]. This example shows that [CONNECTION TO THE THESIS].

3

让步选择链

★★★★★

Admittedly, [OTHER OPTION] can be valuable when [CONDITION]. However, [MY OPTION] is more suitable when [DECISIVE CONDITION], because [REASON].
