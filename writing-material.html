PART 01

最高优先级｜跨题材主链

A

提高效率与分配资源

★★★★★

科技、教育、交通、管理、公共服务、线上平台

减少低价值步骤 → 释放资源 → 投入重要任务 → 效率提高

One major advantage of [CHOICE] is that it reduces the time and effort required to [GOAL]. When [MECHANISM], people can spend less time on [LOW-VALUE TASK] and devote more attention to [HIGHER-VALUE ACTIVITY]. This not only improves immediate efficiency but also makes limited resources easier to manage. Therefore, [CHOICE] is especially valuable when time, money, or staff are limited.

B

实践、技能与成长

★★★★★

课程、实习、兼职、社团、旅行、职业培训

实际行动 → 应用知识 → 掌握技能 → 获得信心

[ACTIVITY] gives people repeated opportunities to apply what they have learned in a real setting. By [ACTION], they learn how to [SKILL] instead of merely memorizing abstract information. As their competence improves, they become more confident and more willing to handle difficult tasks. Over time, this creates a productive cycle of practice, feedback, and improvement.

C

兴趣、动力与正向循环

★★★★★

教育方法、职业选择、员工培训、儿童学习、社团

兴趣 → 持续参与 → 练习增加 → 看见进步 → 动力增强

People are more likely to remain engaged when they can see a clear connection between their effort and a meaningful result. Regular participation gives them more opportunities to practice, while visible progress strengthens their confidence. That confidence then motivates them to continue even when the task becomes difficult. As a result, [CHOICE] is more likely to produce lasting improvement than an approach based only on external pressure.

D

反馈、合作与提高质量

★★★★★

教师指导、团队合作、同伴学习、专家评价、培训

不同视角 → 发现盲点 → 及时纠错 → 质量与效率提高

Working with [PEOPLE] exposes individuals to perspectives and problems they may overlook on their own. Specific feedback helps them identify weaknesses early and adjust their approach before the same mistake becomes a habit. As a result, the quality of their work improves, and less time is wasted on unnecessary revision. This is especially valuable when a task is complex and requires careful judgment.

E

前期投入与长期稳定

★★★★★

教育投资、环境、基础设施、培训、健康、安全

前期投入 → 解决根源 → 避免反复 → 减少未来损失

Although [CHOICE] may require more time or money at first, it can prevent the same problem from occurring repeatedly. By identifying [RISK] early and addressing its underlying cause, [GROUP] can avoid more serious damage and higher costs later. Therefore, the initial investment should be viewed as a preventive measure. In the long run, it creates greater stability and more lasting benefits.

F

信息、判断与避免错误

★★★★★

互联网、新闻、广告、职业、消费、公共决策

可靠信息 → 比较选择 → 预判结果 → 减少错误

Access to reliable information allows people to compare alternatives and understand the likely consequences of each choice. Instead of acting on habit or incomplete assumptions, they can select the option that best matches their needs. This reduces avoidable mistakes and makes decisions easier to explain. Over time, better-informed choices can improve both individual outcomes and public trust.

PART 02

第二优先级｜题材匹配时调用

G

可及性、公平与机会

★★★★

网络教育、交通、补贴、医疗、住房、公共设施

降低门槛 → 获得资源 → 改善能力 → 缩小机会差距

Making [RESOURCE] more accessible allows people with limited time, money, or geographic mobility to benefit from opportunities that might otherwise remain out of reach. Once they gain access, they can develop [SKILL] or receive [SERVICE]. This improves their ability to participate in school, work, or community life. Wider participation can gradually narrow existing gaps and produce broader social benefits.

H

健康、专注与持续表现

★★★★

饮食、运动、休闲、绿地、作息、屏幕使用

压力减少 → 注意力提高 → 错误减少 → 避免长期倦怠

[HABIT OR POLICY] can reduce stress and make a healthy routine easier to maintain. When people are well rested and emotionally balanced, they can concentrate for longer periods, make fewer mistakes, and respond to difficulties more calmly. These immediate improvements also reduce the risk of burnout over time. Therefore, supporting well-being can improve both health and sustained performance.

I

沟通、信任与合作

★★★★

家庭、学校、社团、团队、社区、公司

交流 → 理解需求 → 建立信任 → 合作效率提高

Regular interaction gives people opportunities to exchange information, understand one another’s needs, and correct misunderstandings before they become serious. As trust develops, participants are more willing to share responsibility and cooperate on difficult tasks. This reduces conflict and makes coordination more efficient. Over time, [ACTIVITY OR POLICY] can strengthen both personal relationships and the effectiveness of the wider group.

J

创新、试错与新方案

★★★★

AI、科技、企业冒险、改革、创业、研究

尝试新方法 → 暴露问题 → 根据反馈改进 → 形成新方案

Allowing carefully controlled experimentation gives people or organizations a chance to test ideas that traditional methods may overlook. Even when an early attempt fails, it can reveal useful information about what needs to be changed. That information supports better decisions in the next round. Therefore, strategic risk-taking can lead to innovation, provided that the possible harm is limited and the results are reviewed carefully.

K

环保行为的累积效果

★★★

公共交通、回收、能源、企业政策、消费选择

单次影响小 → 大量人重复 → 效果累积 → 形成更多习惯

Each individual action may appear small, but its effect becomes significant when it is repeated by many people over time. [ACTION] reduces [WASTE, EMISSIONS, OR RESOURCE USE] directly and makes people more aware of the environmental consequences of everyday choices. That awareness can encourage additional responsible habits. As a result, a simple policy can produce both immediate savings and long-term environmental benefits.

L

文化、历史与身份认同

★★★

历史建筑、博物馆、艺术、传统文化、旅游

接触文化实体 → 理解历史 → 身份认同 → 教育和旅游价值

Historical sites and cultural programs give people a tangible connection to the experiences and values of earlier generations. By seeing [OBJECT OR PRACTICE] in its original context, visitors can understand history more vividly than they could through abstract descriptions alone. This strengthens cultural identity and can also support local tourism. However, preservation should focus on places with clear educational, cultural, or economic value.

PART 03

三个论证发动机｜必须背

1

因果链

★★★★★

The main reason is that [CAUSE]. When [CONDITION], [MECHANISM], which means [IMMEDIATE RESULT]. Over time, this can [LARGER CONSEQUENCE].

2

例证链

★★★★★

For example, [PERSON, SCHOOL, COMPANY, OR CITY] recently [ACTION]. Because [RELEVANT DETAIL], [OBSERVABLE RESULT]. This example shows that [CONNECTION TO THE THESIS].

3

让步选择链

★★★★★

Admittedly, [OTHER OPTION] can be valuable when [CONDITION]. However, [MY OPTION] is more suitable when [DECISIVE CONDITION], because [REASON].

一篇只选一条主链：明确立场 ＋ 一条因果链 ＋ 一个具体例子 ＋ 一句回扣；需要时再加一句让步。
