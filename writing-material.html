★★★★★

PART 01

9条主链｜按最近两个月高频排序

A

制度、规模与累积影响

★★★★★

环境、城市、政府政策、快时尚、企业责任、社会规范

单次影响有限 → 制度降低行动难度 → 大量参与 → 形成持久改变

A single action may appear small, but its effect becomes significant when it is repeated by many people or supported by a large institution. Clear rules, practical support, or economic incentives make responsible behavior easier to maintain. As participation expands, the results accumulate and can create broader and more durable change.

B

文化、表达与长期记忆

★★★★★

艺术、文学、讲故事、历史、公共艺术、传统与文化多样性

具体文化载体 → 抽象信息变生动 → 情感与记忆增强 → 促进理解

Art, stories, traditions, and historical places make abstract experiences more vivid and emotionally meaningful. They help people remember shared values and allow different generations or communities to understand one another. Over time, [ACTIVITY] can preserve cultural identity while encouraging reflection and personal expression.

C

信息、反馈与决策质量

★★★★★

商业、新闻、算法、广告、顾客反馈、消费、领导方式

获得可靠信息 → 发现盲点 → 及时调整 → 减少错误

Reliable information and specific feedback help people notice problems they may overlook. Once they understand the strengths and weaknesses of each option, they can adjust before the same mistake becomes a habit. This reduces avoidable errors and improves the quality of their decisions.

D

实践、动力与个人成长

★★★★★

教育、项目学习、实习、职业、体育、员工培训

实际参与 → 应用知识 → 看见进步 → 信心和动力增强

[ACTIVITY] gives people repeated opportunities to apply what they have learned. By [ACTION], they develop practical skills, see visible progress, and gain confidence. This creates a productive cycle of practice, improvement, and motivation.

E

沟通、信任与合作

★★★★★

企业、团队、父母教育、课堂、社区、情商、品牌忠诚

持续交流 → 理解需求 → 建立信任 → 冲突减少、合作提高

Regular interaction allows people to exchange ideas, understand one another’s needs, and correct misunderstandings early. As trust develops, they become more willing to share responsibility and cooperate. This reduces conflict and makes coordination more efficient.

F

效率与资源重新分配

★★★★

自动化、商业、远程工作、城市、交通、公共服务

减少低价值步骤 → 释放时间或资金 → 投入重要任务 → 效率提高

[CHOICE] reduces the time, money, or effort required to [GOAL]. When [MECHANISM], people can spend fewer resources on [LOW-VALUE TASK] and devote more attention to [HIGHER-VALUE ACTIVITY]. This improves efficiency and makes limited resources easier to manage.

G

创新、适应与风险控制

★★★★

AI、自动化、算法、监控、产品设计、企业创新

受控试验 → 暴露问题 → 调整方案 → 保留收益并限制风险

Carefully controlled experimentation allows people to test solutions that traditional methods may overlook. Even when an early attempt fails, it reveals what needs to change and supports a better decision in the next round. Innovation can create lasting progress when risks are limited and results are reviewed carefully.

H

公平、可及性与机会

★★★★

社会流动、最低工资、UBI、在线教育、住房、公共设施

降低门槛 → 获得资源 → 能力与参与提高 → 机会差距缩小

Making [RESOURCE] more accessible allows people with limited time, money, or mobility to reach opportunities that would otherwise remain unavailable. Once they gain access, they can develop skills, receive essential services, and participate more fully in society. Wider participation can narrow existing gaps and improve social mobility.

I

安全感、健康与持续表现

★★★★

远程工作、工作生活平衡、最低工资、UBI、健康、职业选择

安全感增加 → 压力下降 → 专注和判断改善 → 长期表现稳定

[CHOICE] reduces stress and provides the stability people need to focus on long-term goals. When people feel physically, emotionally, or financially secure, they can concentrate better, make fewer rushed decisions, and respond to difficulties more calmly. This improves both quality of life and sustained performance.

压缩结论：D合并原“实践”与“兴趣”，C合并原“反馈”与“信息判断”，A合并原“环保累积”与“制度规模”；其余链的独立机制不同，继续合并会让论证变空，因此保留9条。

PART 02

只背4个通用例子

例1

大学小组项目／混合协作

★★★★★

A student team divided the research and speaking tasks and held brief check-ins. One member questioned an unreliable source, while another reorganized a confusing section. Because everyone could give feedback, the team finished on time and produced a clearer presentation.

可套：项目学习、领导方式、课堂参与、父母参与、从众、社交媒体学习、远程工作、情商。

例2

大学图书馆／本地书店

★★★★★

A university library introduced self-checkout machines and asked students for feedback. After the screen was simplified, routine loans became faster, so staff could help students find reliable sources. A part-time assistant also received predictable pay and could work accurately without taking extra night shifts.

可套：自动化、顾客反馈、本地企业、信息判断、财商、最低工资、品牌忠诚、公共服务。

例3

学校能源审计＋维修回收

★★★★★

A student team checked empty classrooms and found lights and computers left on overnight. The school tested an automatic shutoff system and organized repair days for backpacks and small devices. Electricity use and discarded products both declined, and the students improved the plan after reviewing the results.

可套：环保教育、个人环保行动、政府政策、快时尚、企业责任、创新、城市发展。

例4

社区博物馆＋公共壁画

★★★★★

A city museum invited local artists and older residents to create a mural about immigrant families. They combined family photographs, traditional colors, and short personal stories. Younger visitors could understand experiences they had never lived through and remember the history more clearly.

可套：艺术作用、文学、讲故事、文化多样性、公共艺术、历史人物、全球化与传统。

换题原则：尽量不换整个例子，只替换人物身份、行动细节和可观察结果。例如把“student team”换成“employees”，把“presentation”换成“product design”。

GTRT 59 ⭐ Environmental Action

制度与累积

题目：步行、少买并维修、减少用电等行动中，哪一种最有效？

  1. I agree with Andrew that reusing items can reduce waste, but I would add that repairing products is even more effective because it prevents waste before it is created.

    思考：先回应Andrew，再增加“维修发生在废物产生之前”这个新观点。｜来源：固定开头。

  2. A single repair may appear small, but its effect becomes significant when many people repeat it.

    思考：从个人小行动过渡到规模效应。｜来源：A链第1句。

  3. For example, our school recently organized a repair day for backpacks and small electronic devices.

    思考：直接调用学校环保通用例子。｜来源：例3＋例证链。

  4. Because volunteers fixed broken zippers and replaced phone batteries, dozens of products remained useful instead of being discarded.

    思考：必须给出两个动作和一个可观察结果。｜来源：例证链。

  5. This also avoided the raw materials, manufacturing energy, and transportation required for replacements.

    思考：把“少扔垃圾”扩展到生产和运输环节，使论证更完整。｜来源：A链的累积结果。

  6. Claire is right that reducing electricity use can lower emissions.

    思考：承认另一种做法也有价值。

  7. However, repair affects several stages of production at once, so it creates broader and more durable environmental benefits.

    思考：用“影响范围更广”完成比较并回扣立场。｜来源：让步选择链。
